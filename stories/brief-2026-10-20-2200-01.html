Witkoff meets Putin Wednesday after "constructive" Oslo talks — framework covers Article 5-like guarantees

A US official called Tuesday's Oslo talks "constructive" with "meaningful progress" toward a coordinated approach to engaging Russia. The framework covers security guarantees for Ukraine (Zelensky confirmed "Article 5-like" guarantees were discussed and thanked the US for underlining they "will be strong"), a post-war economic plan, and a broader European security architecture. Witkoff flies to St. Petersburg on Wednesday aiming to open a path to direct trilateral talks. Trump, meanwhile, pressed Kyiv to settle: "Ukraine has to want to settle this war," adding that Moscow wants a deal. Zelensky said Ukraine is once again receiving US early warnings of Russian missile attacks — "Now it works, it's great" — after a call with Rubio where Patriot interceptors and Starlink/Starshield were also discussed.
