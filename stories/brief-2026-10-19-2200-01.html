Night brief · 1. World escalation

Saudi Arabia postponed its flagship investment summit to 2027

Saudi Arabia postponed its flagship investment summit to 2027. The Future Investment Initiative ("Davos in the Desert," scheduled Oct 26–29 in Riyadh) was canceled Monday amid the Houthi missile wave. JPMorgan and Citigroup bankers were already canceling meetings; Dimon, Fraser, Solomon, and Fink were all on the speaker list. A clear sign the security situation is biting the kingdom's economic ambitions, not just its airspace.
