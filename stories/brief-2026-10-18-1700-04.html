Lithuania drone incident follow-up (developing)

  • Lithuania drone incident follow-up (developing) — Ukraine's Foreign Ministry spokesperson Heorhii Tykhyi apologized and said the incident may have been caused by Russian electronic warfare diverting a Ukrainian drone off course; Lithuania's crisis-management chief says it was most likely a Ukrainian drone that strayed during the overnight strikes on Russia's St. Petersburg region. The defense minister's claim that it entered from Poland is now disputed — Poland's Defense Ministry says its radar did not confirm any such crossing, and Lithuania asked Poland to join a joint investigation. President Nausėda: "Russia's war of aggression against Ukraine is spilling over onto the eastern flank of NATO and EU." Origin still unconfirmed. Reuters via LaPost · EuropeSays · NewspotNG
