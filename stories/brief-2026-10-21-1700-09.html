Apple confirms "limited" iPhone Duo walk-in stock for Friday — and Gurman calls the preorder timing a "major mistake"

Apple told MacRumors and 9to5Mac that stores will have limited Duo units for walk-in customers on Friday's Oct. 23 launch in the US, Australia, Austria, Belgium, Canada, Mexico, Germany, the Netherlands, Singapore, Spain, Sweden, Switzerland, Türkiye, the UK and most stores in Italy (Macao: launch-day walk-ins only, limited). No walk-in sales in France, China, Hong Kong, India, Japan, Malaysia, South Korea, Taiwan, Thailand or the UAE — reservation-only pickup there. Apple expects the limited stock to sell out quickly; AppleInsider reports new pre-orders are now showing January 25, 2027 delivery. Bloomberg's Mark Gurman, in his Power On newsletter, called launching pre-orders Oct. 16 despite known "severe supply constraints" a "major mistake."
