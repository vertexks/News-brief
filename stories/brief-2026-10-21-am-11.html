Nvidia GTC Berlin keynote: OpenShell to leash AI agents, DSX POD factories in 14 days, Vera Rubin in full production

Jensen Huang's keynote Wednesday unveiled the OpenShell Reference Architecture — models think on Vera Rubin systems, agent tool-use runs on Vera CPUs, and BlueField-4 chips run Sentry software to watch agent behavior from outside the system and enforce policy in hardware — plus DSX POD, prebuilt container AI factories Nvidia says cut onsite deployment from six months to 14 days, with deliveries worldwide in early 2027. Nvidia says Vera Rubin is in full production, its European commitments now approach $10 billion, and its DSX designs can cut cooling water use by up to 100%. The Register's caveat, noted in coverage: OpenShell requires Nvidia hardware, still has open security issues on GitHub, and collects telemetry by default unless users opt out. Separately, Nvidia announced an Isaac robotics expansion — a reference humanoid (Agile ONE SN, 1.40m, 31 degrees of freedom, Jetson Thor onboard), Isaac OS runtime and Isaac Lab 3.0 — and supply-chain capacity expansion including 2.5x substrate capacity over three years.
