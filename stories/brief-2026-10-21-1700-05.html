Iran–US deadlock, stated plainly by both sides: no talks under blockade, no relief while ships are attacked

Iranian President Pezeshkian said Wednesday the US "cannot expect dialogue" while imposing a blockade and issuing threats. Foreign Minister Araghchi, in a Financial Times op-ed, said Iran would give diplomacy "another chance" if it sees "strong evidence of a sincere desire" for a just solution — and urged a return to the June Islamabad memorandum, under which Iran would reopen the strait and the US would lift the blockade, release frozen oil funds and remove sanctions. Washington isn't biting: Secretary of State Rubio told US media a deal would be "extremely difficult" and economic pressure continues until Iran stops targeting commercial shipping. Indirect backchannels via Pakistan and Qatar are reportedly still active, but nothing is confirmed. Bloomberg via Military.com · AnewZ
