Murdaugh retrial (Oct 16 hearing outcomes): defense won the DNA-testing fight but lost the ballistics bid. Judge McCaslin allowed defense Y-STR testing (by Othram, ~90 days) of Maggie Murdaugh's phone swabs and fingernail clippings even though it will consume nearly all remaining testable material — but rejected broader ballistic-data requests. She also corrected the record: Murdaugh FAILED the polygraph Harpootlian claimed he passed "with flying colors" (it concerned financial crimes). Warned both sides proceedings won't "become a circus." Discovery fight over police handling of the murder weapons was not argued Friday. Next: Nov 13 hearing, Jan 20 2027 Sumter hearing, jury notices February, retrial Apr 5, 2027.
