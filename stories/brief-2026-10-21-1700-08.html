Google Cloud's Vera Rubin A5X instances go live — general availability in November, up to 960,000 Rubin GPUs planned

Announced at GTC Berlin on Wednesday: the first live A5X instances, built on Nvidia Vera Rubin NVL72 racks with ConnectX-9 SuperNICs, Google's Virgo Network and fourth-generation liquid cooling, are running with an initial customer — Ineffable Intelligence, the London lab founded by AlphaGo creator David Silver, which is using them for reinforcement-learning systems that learn from experience. General availability comes next month, with expansion to Europe planned, and Google Cloud says it plans to scale Vera Rubin infrastructure to as many as 960,000 GPUs across multiple sites (a stated plan, not installed capacity). WPP also demoed a creative-agent reference architecture on A5X with Nvidia Nemotron models and OpenShell, with multi-node workloads running on a single instance in initial tests.
