Russell Brand trial: final complainant's evidence concludes — jury sent home until Thursday

At Southwark Crown Court on Wednesday, the final complainant (Woman 5) completed her evidence. Under cross-examination she maintained she did not consent to the alleged 2009 anal rape, and on re-examination by prosecutor Riel Karmy-Jones KC she answered "Absolutely not" when asked whether having anal sex with Brand was a decision she made — "That was the whole reason for going to the police." She told jurors Brand "acted like nothing had happened" afterward. The jury was sent home until Thursday morning. Brand denies all seven charges (3 rapes, 3 sexual assaults, 1 indecent assault, 1999–2009); the trial continues. The Times live blog
