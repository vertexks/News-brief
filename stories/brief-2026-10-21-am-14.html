Nvidia DeepStream 9.2: more of it open source, smarter frames for vision-language models

DeepStream 9.2 is live, and more of the SDK is now open source — the monorepo includes 12 GStreamer elements and 30+ libraries as source, including codecs and Re-ID vision/text embedding plugins. New: intelligent frame selection (send only the most relevant frames to VLMs), an active-learning pipeline with runtime model switching, and GB300 support plus JetPack 7.2.1 for Jetson Thor/Orin.
