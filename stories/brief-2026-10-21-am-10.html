Oil back above $110 as talks rumours fade and US stocks draw down

Oil back above $110 as talks rumours fade and US stocks draw down

Brent rose 1.5% to $110.38 in early Wednesday trading (WTI $99.18) after settling Tuesday at $108.79 off a five-month high of $111.99. Tuesday's dip came on unconfirmed reports of Qatar- and Pakistan-brokered mediation — Pakistan's army chief met Iranian leaders in Tehran on Oct 20 — but Iranian outlet Nournews reported Iran saying there would be no talks until its demands are met, and no official confirmation of a Qatari delegation emerged. API data showed US crude stocks fell 4.9 million barrels last week, on top of an 11.12 million draw the week before. Araghchi reiterated the strait stays closed until Washington accepts Tehran's seven-day plan; indirect talks via Pakistan and Qatar are reported to be continuing, but nothing is confirmed.
