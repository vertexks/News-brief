1. California Department of Education breach — millions of students' data stolen via ETS contractor

A hacker used a compromised login to access Educational Testing Service's SFTP site (state testing data exchange) and copied files with names, birth dates, addresses and disability status of "all students enrolled in the three previous school years" — ~7M public school students. Activity ran May 22–Sep 18, 2026; identified Sep 25. No SSNs or financial data accessed; ETS says no evidence of public release so far.
