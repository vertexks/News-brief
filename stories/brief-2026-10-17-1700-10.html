Nvidia denied RTX 5090 discontinuation rumors

Nvidia denied RTX 5090 discontinuation rumors. SVP Jeff Fisher, at the RTX Spark Taipei launch party, said the flagship GPU "will continue shipping" but acknowledged severe supply constraints — cards are already $5,000+ in most regions, and the RTX 5080 24GB is reportedly set to become the new flagship.

Source: Wccftech (https://wccftech.com/nvidia-denies-rtx-5090-discontinuation-reports/)
