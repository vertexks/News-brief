Follow-up: PHP2Shell exploitation is now being observed in the wild — still no KEV listing

The evening brief's patch-now warning got sharper overnight: watchTowr says it is now observing in-the-wild exploitation attempts against the PHP2Shell flaws (CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619, both CVSS 9.2), with a variety of hastily built proof-of-concept exploits hitting its sensors — none successful yet, in its assessment, but "we don't expect that will last long." CISA issued an overnight alert urging immediate patching to 8.5.12 / 8.4.27 / 8.3.36 / 8.2.35, and Australia's cyber centre issued an urgent warning late Oct. 21. The scale problem: roughly 79–80% of PHP sites run versions that no longer receive support, so for them there is no official patch — only upgrade, isolation, or a WAF as a stopgap. Neither CVE was in CISA's KEV catalog in the reports checked. Cyber Daily · SC Media
