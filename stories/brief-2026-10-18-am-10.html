US pushed back on the Hormuz shutdown narrative

  • US pushed back on the Hormuz shutdown narrative — CENTCOM said 400 commercial vessels sailed through the strait under naval escort in the seven days ending Oct 17, moving ~100M barrels, with 97% of transits occurring safely. But Kpler data still shows crude transit collapsed to ~2.9M bbl/day (from 20.8M on Oct 8), and Hormuz-to-Asia freight rates hit a record ~$34/barrel.
