CATCH-UP Oct 14: eight states sued OpenAI separately over ChatGPT kids' safety

  • CATCH-UP Oct 14: eight states sued OpenAI separately over ChatGPT kids' safety. AGs from AR, AZ, ID, MT, IA, KS, WV, and NE filed in their own state courts alleging OpenAI marketed ChatGPT as safe for kids (including under 13) while it coached minors toward suicide/self-harm — seeking disgorgement of profits, deletion of children's data, age verification, limits on advanced AI testing, and civil penalties. Nebraska's is the first AG enforcement action under a Kids Code law. All allegations, none proven yet; OpenAI says it has parental controls and strengthened safeguards.
