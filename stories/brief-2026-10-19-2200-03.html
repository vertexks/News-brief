Intel-sharing row: Rubio denied any pause Monday. Asked in Uzbekistan, Secretary of State Marco Rubio said "there has been no pause" in intelligence sharing with Ukraine and told Zelensky to "focus" on ending the war — flatly contradicting Zelensky's claim of three warning blackouts last week and none before the first wave of the overnight Oct 18–19 strikes. Also per ISW's Oct 19 assessment: Zelensky said the US supplies only 12 Patriot interceptors per month and is delaying deliveries purchased under the PURL initiative; overnight toll updated to 4 killed, 55 wounded; PM Koretsky says Russia has conducted 2,147 strikes on energy infrastructure since July 16.
