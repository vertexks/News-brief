Third tanker mine claim in Hormuz — still Iran-only, unverified

  • Third tanker mine claim in Hormuz — still Iran-only, unverified. The IRGC Navy says another oil tanker struck a naval mine Sunday evening in the strait's southern reaches (engine-room explosion and fire, vessel immobilized), the third such claim since Saturday. No vessel names, no flags, no casualty info, no independent confirmation — same pattern as the two Saturday claims. Iran FM Araqchi on Sunday reiterated Tehran's offer to reopen the strait within days, and acknowledged a US response to the proposal. Developing, treat as single-source.
