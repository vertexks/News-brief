1. World escalation

Houthis hit Yemen's presidential palace complex in Aden — missiles struck near the Maashiq compound, seat of the internationally recognized government; PM and cabinet survived; health minister says 5 killed, 16 wounded. Reuters and AP carried separate accounts. First time the Houthis have gone after Aden's seat of government.

Saudi coalition says it intercepted a cruise missile in the Medina region; Houthis deny any attacks today — coalition spox Turki al-Maliki announced the interception; Houthi spox Saree called the Medina claim a lie. Neither the missile's origin nor attribution is established — developing.
