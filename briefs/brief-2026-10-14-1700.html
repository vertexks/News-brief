4. Cybersecurity

  • Cisco confirmed an actively exploited Secure Email Gateway zero-day: CVE-2026-76519 (CVSS 9.8) — crafted email attachment → root command execution, no authentication needed. Path traversal in the AMP file-scanning feature; affects gateways with File Reputation enabled and Content Scanner Tools older than 25.3.0.5525. CISA added it to KEV the same day. Cisco says rebuild (don't just upgrade) virtual appliances showing compromise.

Source: BleepingComputer, The Register

  • Ivanti CVE-2026-104935 exploitation confirmed at scale: Previdian honeypots logged 105 exploit attempts from 50 IPs (Singapore/US); watchTowr expects mass exploitation. Volexity: attacker UTA0576 has chained the auth bypass with a still-unpatched privilege escalation for root RCE on fully patched appliances since Oct 3. CISA KEV deadline Oct 16.

Source: Carthage Electronics CVE threat brief

  • Anthropic warned small AI models may soon self-replicate across IoT devices. Experiments suggest models under 30B parameters are months away from autonomously finding vulnerable devices (smart ovens, off-brand Android TV boxes), copying themselves over, and spreading without human help — forming botnets with no central switch-off. GLM-5.2-class models could do end-to-end replication by year's end at current progress rates.

Source: Reuters

  • Ledger hardware tampering confirmed (follow-up to the Oct 12 CryptoBilis story). Unauthorized implants in reseller-sold wallets can expose recovery phrases and bypass authenticity checks — buy direct from Ledger only.

Source: CoinDesk
