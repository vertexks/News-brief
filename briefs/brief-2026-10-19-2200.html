(AP via usa-today-news.com: https://usa-today-news.com/news/saudi-arabia-responds-to-drone-and-cruise-missile-attacks-and-other-mideast-developments/)

4. Cybersecurity

NEW: Siemens Software Center vulnerability exploited in the wild. Siemens confirmed CVE-2026-110936 (CVSS 8.4) on Oct 19: a DLL-sideloading flaw in Software Center used by a ClickFix campaign that bundles a genuine, signed copy of the program with malicious DLLs — "the Siemens program was not modified," so it inherits the trusted signature. Fixed in 4.0.9.8; no Siemens systems breached. (securityonline.info: https://securityonline.info/siemens-software-center-cve-2026-110936-exploited/)

CrowdStrike: unknown actor is using the Chinese AI pentest tool ARTEX against South Korean banks. Operational deployment of an agentic attack framework doing autonomous recon, exploitation, and lateral movement against financial institutions — comes after bank CEOs apologized to the National Assembly over a major breach. ARTEX (33k-ish stars, Chinese-developed) has crossed from GitHub demo to real-world weapon. (cybersecnews.org: https://www.cybersecnews.org/)

Unpatched Broadcom Wi-Fi vulnerability: attackers within wireless range can execute code with root privileges on affected routers — no CVE assigned, no patch as of Oct 19. (cybersecnews.org: https://www.cybersecnews.org/)

BeyondTrust zero-day: federal patch deadline was TODAY. CVE-2026-102764 (CVSS 9.3/9.8) in Remote Support, exploited by China's Armory Castle since Aug 29. CISA KEV deadline Oct 19 for it and Fortinet CVE-2025-61624; Rapid7 published an emergency response write-up urging forensic triage for prior compromise. (SC Media: https://www.scworld.com/brief/cisa-adds-fortinet-and-beyondtrust-vulnerabilities-to-exploited-list , https://www.rapid7.com/blog/post/etr-cve-2026-102764-critical-beyondtrust-remote-support-authentication-bypass-exploited-in-the-wild/)
