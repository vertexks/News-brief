4. Cybersecurity

  • China-linked Armory Castle (UNC5221/Warp Panda) exploited a BeyondTrust zero-day (CVE-2026-102764, CVSS 9.3) in late August to hijack a Remote Support server; improper origin validation allowed remote file operations. SaaS customers patched Oct 9; self-hosted (24.3.1–26.2.3) must apply BT26-05-RS manually. (The Hacker News via nationalcybersecurity)
  • PwnEscape (CVE-2026-99999, CVSS 9.3): guest VMs can read host memory. Linux kernel networking-stack flaw found by pwn.ai's Paulos Yibelo in Vercel's $1M sandbox challenge; Vercel confirmed the leak on live production sandboxes. No sign of real-world exploitation. Affects the shared kernel layer cloud providers rely on — big one. (The Hacker News)
  • Citizen Lab: zero-click Candiru spyware delivered via online ads ("AdFection"). Israeli firm Discover-Tech's ad infrastructure distributed Candiru spyware (now "Integrity Labs") that infected 45 Al Jazeera Windows devices in 2024–25; malicious ads seen across hundreds of sensitive networks incl. the Pentagon, IAEA, and ICC. (Devdiscourse/Reuters)
  • Ahold Delhaize: websites/apps offline for a 5th day. Dutch Justice Minister called on Ahold to file a criminal complaint; company says no customer/associate/partner data was taken; Albert Heijn doesn't expect online delivery back before Sunday. Physical stores open. (Reuters)
  • CISA federal patch deadline TODAY (Oct 16) for Ivanti Connect Secure CVE-2026-104935 (auth bypass → root RCE, actively exploited by UTA0576). (Security Magazine)
