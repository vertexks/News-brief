1. Reflection releases Beam open weights under Apache 2.0

501B-total / 23B-active-per-token MoE text model with a 1M-token context window, built for coding/agentic tasks with an adjustable reasoning-effort parameter. Self-hostable; Baseten announced day-0 support ($1.30/$0.13 per 1M input/output tokens). Reported benchmarks: 90.5% GPQA Diamond, 80.9% SWE-bench Verified, 97.8% AIME 2026 — those are vendor-claimed, treat accordingly.

Unite.AI

2. Shelly ALPM 3.1.7 ships RLPM — a native Zig rewrite of Arch's libalpm

The Arch package manager Shelly (GUI+CLI, links directly against libalpm rather than wrapping pacman) quietly shipped RLPM in its Oct 16 patch release: a Zig reimplementation of libalpm's full workflow — repo reads, dependency resolution, verification/downloads, hooks/scriptlets, installs, DB updates — running as a parallel backend with sealed memfd archives and Landlock/seccomp sandboxing. Author notes it's not finished — mark experimental.

1. California Department of Education breach — millions of students' data stolen via ETS contractor

A hacker used a compromised login to access Educational Testing Service's SFTP site (state testing data exchange) and copied files with names, birth dates, addresses and disability status of "all students enrolled in the three previous school years" — ~7M public school students. Activity ran May 22–Sep 18, 2026; identified Sep 25. No SSNs or financial data accessed; ETS says no evidence of public release so far.
