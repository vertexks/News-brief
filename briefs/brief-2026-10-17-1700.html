1. World escalation

Trump waived the Graham Act Russia sanctions a day before they took effect. Executive order signed Oct 16 waived most of the Lindsey O. Graham Sanctioning Russia and Iran Act's new measures — up to 500% tariffs on Russian goods and 100% duties on the five biggest buyers of Russian oil/gas — days before the Oct 18 deadline; older sanctions stay. Blumenthal accused Trump of turning his back on Congress; Dmitriev praised it as "historic."
