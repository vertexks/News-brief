1. World escalation

Trump announces an "energy ceasefire" — Kyiv says it was news to them, Moscow silent, Russia keeps striking

Trump posted Sunday morning that an energy ceasefire between Russia and Ukraine is "effective immediately" and both sides agreed. Zelensky said Ukraine supports it but is still awaiting details from Washington, and that he learned of it from the announcement. No Russian reaction yet. In his nightly address Zelensky said Russian attacks on energy sites continued, including a hit on a combined heat-and-power plant in Kharkiv, and counted 200+ strikes on generating capacity in the past week. Developing — no confirmed terms or start time.

Reuters reports a fresh projectile hit King Khalid International Airport Terminal 4 on Sunday, sparking a fire and injuring an unspecified number; Saudi air defenses also intercepted a cruise missile nearby. Third tanker hits a mine in Hormuz in two days; UKMTO reports a second projectile strike Sunday; 22 crew rescued off Ras Al Khaimah

Iran's IRGC says a crude supertanker on an "unauthorized" southern route with navigation systems off struck a naval mine Sunday and caught fire — the third such mine incident since Saturday — and warns ships with ID systems off will be treated as hostile. 4. Cybersecurity

FBI contractor removed after breach traced to an unpatched Oracle PeopleSoft flaw (CVE-2026-35273)

SC Media reports the FBI confirmed a third-party platform failure led to a breach affecting thousands of employees: a contractor never applied a critical PeopleSoft patch, and ShinyHunters exploited it, adapting to bypass existing defenses. Verizon's 2026 DBIR is cited for vuln exploitation now being the top initial-access vector. This is the breach side of the ShinyHunters case whose third arrest was in this morning's brief.

Source: https://www.scworld.com/brief/fbi-contractor-removed-after-data-breach-due-to-unapplied-security-patch

Catch-up (Oct 4, not previously reported): Double Counter Discord bot breach — ~28M accounts' data treated as exposed

Tellter SAS, operator of the Double Counter verification bot used in 600,000+ Discord communities, confirmed attackers entered via a retired OVH server still running a public Metabase instance, stole cloud credentials, and copied ~12 GB in about 25 minutes: ~28M Discord user IDs/usernames, ~27M IP addresses with coarse geolocation, and ~1M email addresses. The bot token was hijacked to post links in ~50 large servers and a stolen payment key was used for $7,316 in fraud. Discord disabled new installs while reviewing. Have I Been Pwned has loaded a 274,922-email public subset.

Sources: https://breachhistory.com/blog/double-counter-discord-bot-breach-2026 | https://en.softonic.com/articles/double-counter-breach-leaks-ip-data-from-28-million-discord-accounts

New CVE Oct 11: databasement RCE (CVE-2026-108963, CVSS 8.8)

Versions before 1.8.2 of the databasement backup tool allow authenticated remote code execution via a crafted database name passed to dump commands. No confirmed public exploit and not in CISA KEV — patch to 1.8.2 if you run it.

Source: https://www.thehackerwire.com/vulnerability/CVE-2026-108963/
