5. Cybersecurity

Plex2Shell actively exploited in the wild

Chain of 4 CVEs (CVE-2026-96652/96654/96655/96656) gives unauthenticated RCE on Plex Media Server; PoC published Oct 16, attacks started Oct 17. Payloads: XMRig miner ("mylovelyminer") + Linux proxy backdoor ("myawesomeproxy") linked to AISURU botnet infra. Fix: 1.43.3.10861+ (patched in August — the victims are unpatched servers). Worth checking any Plex install. XDA · neotechcloudsolutions

OpenCode Console breached — attackers stole users' API keys for a "large-scale distillation attack"

Disclosed Oct 17–18: unauthorized access to a legacy system being decommissioned; 1.4% of Console users affected (only those with external providers linked). Victims report unauthorized charges of hundreds to $1,700; Anthropic/Google blocked the exposed keys, OpenAI is refunding. If you run OpenCode Console with linked provider keys, rotate them. TechCrunch · BleepingComputer

AfD email leak: 421,179 internal emails (281 GB) published on hexane.cc

AfD confirmed Oct 19 that a hosting provider was hacked in September and mailboxes copied.
