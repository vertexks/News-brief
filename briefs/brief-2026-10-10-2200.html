🌙 Night brief — Saturday, Oct 10, 2026 (~10 PM CT)

1. World escalation

  • Houthi missile attack hits Riyadh airport — 12 dead, 300+ hurt; Trump says US "may" join Saudi strikes.

The Saudi civil aviation authority says Saturday's strike on King Khalid International Airport killed 12 (one American, four Saudis, plus Egyptian, Jordanian, Palestinian, Sudanese, Bangladeshi and Syrian nationals) and wounded 309 — the deadliest attack on a Gulf Arab country since the war began in February. It was the third airport attack this week; the airport shut down with flights diverted or cancelled. A projectile also fell near Dammam's airport, and Trump told reporters the US could join strikes on the Houthis: "We move very quickly." Developing — figures per Saudi GACA.

Sources: https://nypost.com/2026/10/10/world-news/iran-backed-houthis-strike-riyadh-airport-as-trump-weighs-us-says-response/ and https://www.livemint.com/news/world/riyadh-airport-attack-at-least-12-killed-over-300-injured-trump-says-strikes-not-acceptable-top-updates-11791681437870.html

  • Trump says it's "time for Ukraine to get a new president" in escalating diesel-row with Zelensky.

One day after announcing a plan to buy Russian diesel, Trump told reporters Zelensky should stop hitting Russian refineries ("He better damn well stop") and suggested Ukraine needs "a new leader who can make a deal." Kyiv says it will keep hitting refineries — its main leverage — and Britain/EU rallied behind Zelensky; No. 10 said the UK won't join the diesel deal. Meanwhile Ukrainian officials and US negotiators held peace talks in Miami on Friday; Putin rejected resuming talks, though TASS reports Witkoff and Kushner may visit Moscow within two weeks. Developing.

Sources: https://www.wsj.com/world/trump-calls-on-ukraine-to-stop-hitting-russian-refineriesand-replace-zelensky-cc9bada8 and https://www.thetimes.com/world/russia-ukraine-war/article/ukraine-needs-a-new-leader-trump-says-in-diesel-row-dc957nd0t

  • US disabled a cargo ship running the Iran blockade; Hormuz shipping at its lowest in two months.

CENTCOM said Saturday a US fighter jet struck the stern of the Panama-flagged M/V Ocean Molica (aka "Arika Sun") with a precision munition, disabling its propulsion without harming the crew after it left an Iranian port and ignored blockade warnings. Reuters notes attacks on tankers hit their highest level since the war began in February and strait transits fell to a two-month low. Separately, an outbound tanker was struck by a projectile in the strait today (UKMTO report, fire aboard, crew safe), and Iran's IRGC Navy says the strait stays closed until the US lifts the blockade. Trump says "very good conversations" are happening with Iran but the US won't be "blackmailed" over the waterway. Developing.

Sources: https://www.reuters.com/world/us-military-says-it-disabled-cargo-vessel-trying-run-iran-blockade-2026-10-11/ and https://www.defconlevel.com/alert/2026-10-10-outbound-tanker-struck-by-projectile-in-strait-of-hormuz
