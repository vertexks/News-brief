1. World escalation

  • Ukraine launched one of its largest drone attacks of the war overnight — Russia claims ~1,015 drones downed near Moscow and western regions; St. Petersburg oil terminal hit and burning, 1 killed and 5 injured, ~20,000 without power in the Leningrad region after two substations were struck. St. Petersburg governor confirmed the attacks on Telegram. Fresh Blog News (AFP) · CNN
  • Russia kept up its own overnight barrage Oct 17–18 — 1 Iskander-M missile and 180 attack drones launched, 152 downed per Ukraine's Air Force; Zelensky said Sunday Russia has hit Ukraine's energy and oil/gas sectors 76 times in the week since the US announced the energy ceasefire. Ukrainian authorities report 11 killed and nearly 50 injured in the 24 hours to Sunday morning, including a major attack on energy facilities in Odesa. CNN · korabelov.info
  • Zelensky: Starlink curbs on Ukraine's battlefield drones still biting (follow-up) — said Saturday military access remains curtailed (Starlink cuts out above 161 km/h) and troops "can't currently use Starlink or Starshield any more" to shoot down jet-powered Shaheds. The Trump administration denies restricting the service. Single-source claim vs denial. CNN
  • Lithuania issued a shelter alert Sunday morning after a suspected drone entered its airspace (developing) — residents in central/northern areas told to shelter ~1 hour, Kaunas airport temporarily closed, two NATO Italian Eurofighters scrambled but couldn't visually identify the object in cloudy weather; debris near Juodzionys village reportedly struck a civilian vehicle. Lithuanian defence minister said it likely came from Poland's direction; Poland said its radar did not confirm a border crossing; Latvia issued a brief "yellow" warning in border regions. Reuters wire via 1027wbow
  • Catch-up (Saturday, missed by prior briefs): Romania's F-16s shot down two jet-powered Geran attack drones inside Romanian airspace — targets detected ~120 km east of Snake Island heading toward the coast near Sulina; Romanian MOD confirmed. Intended targets unconfirmed. itamilradar
  • Egypt's parliament to hold an emergency session Sunday to authorize defending Saudi Arabia if Riyadh requests it — el-Sisi: "not fighting anyone or attacking anyone… defence only"; Egypt's National Defence Council had pledged "all necessary support" to Riyadh. Bloomberg via Livemint · Morning Chronicle
  • Saudi-led coalition intercepted a new overnight wave — two drones over Riyadh, one over the Eastern Province, two ballistic missiles aimed at Khamis Mushait; coalition released footage of strikes on Houthi missile launchers. Separately, a Saudi AH-6i helicopter crashed in Najran near the Yemen border, killing two officers — coalition says "technical malfunction," while Houthi spokesman Yahya Saree claimed they shot down an Apache in the same area. Both claims reported, disputed. Morning Chronicle · The Traveler
  • Houthi media reported Saturday that Saudi strikes killed 12 people in Yemen, including three children (unverified, single-source). Morning Chronicle
  • Riyadh extended remote learning for schools by another seven days — the Future Investment Initiative later this month is weighing contingency measures including virtual attendance, per Bloomberg. Indian Embassy in Riyadh issued an emergency safety advisory Saturday night urging nationals to shelter indoors, avoid windows/balconies/rooftops, and not photograph incidents. Bloomberg via Livemint · Daily Jagran
  • US pushed back on the Hormuz shutdown narrative — CENTCOM said 400 commercial vessels sailed through the strait under naval escort in the seven days ending Oct 17, moving ~100M barrels, with 97% of transits occurring safely. But Kpler data still shows crude transit collapsed to ~2.9M bbl/day (from 20.8M on Oct 8), and Hormuz-to-Asia freight rates hit a record ~$34/barrel. Hindu BusinessLine · forexrev/Al Jazeera via Kpler
  • Trump at a Kentucky rally Saturday night: the Iran war will end "very quickly" and gas prices will drop once it does — claimed US strikes "beat the shit out of" Iran and knocked out its nuclear capacity. Iran's deputy FM Khatibzadeh responded that ending hostilities on all fronts is essential for lasting peace; Iran's interior minister traveled to Pakistan — a key mediator — for talks. Indian Economic Observer · bankb.it

  • Samsung to build Nvidia's next-gen memory base dies on TSMC's 3nm (report) — Samsung accepted Nvidia's request to produce base dies for its next-generation custom HBM (NVHBM) on TSMC's 3-nanometer process, per the Seoul Economic Daily (via Reuters, Oct 18); first time Samsung has partnered with TSMC from the HBM manufacturing stage. Nvidia reportedly asked all three NVHBM suppliers (Samsung, SK Hynix, Micron) to standardize on TSMC 3nm base dies. Still a newspaper report, not an official Nvidia/Samsung confirmation. Reuters

Also opened Search and Fetch APIs for agents at $0.75/1,000 calls. Techi
  • vLLM v0.32.0 shipped — 814 commits from 363 contributors; new POST /v1/systemone structured-decisions endpoint, NVFP4 KV cache across more GPU architectures, Model Runner V2 as default on ROCm, plus security fixes (template resource exhaustion GHSA-4hhp-h66f-j5j7 and others, default CORS origins restricted). GitHub releases
  • Obsidian launched "Pele," an open-source Mermaid diagram renderer — renders Mermaid syntax as themeable SVG with zero dependencies; project claims 230 kB gzip bundle (vs 1.6 MB Mermaid.js), 0.05–0.3 ms renders, full compatibility with Mermaid 12.1.0. Benchmark figures are project-provided, not independently verified. YouGoNews
  • LiteLLM v1.106.0 signs all Docker images with cosign — users can now verify image signatures via the key introduced in commit 0112e53. olud.ai
  • Telegraf 1.40 released — adds support for Databricks Delta tables and an official Zerobus pathway to stream live metrics into storage tables. saudishopper
  • Rust clean-room CAD/design apps trending today — CADCraft (open-source AutoCAD-style app in pure Rust, +202 stars today) and FigCraft (Figma-style collaborative design reimplementation in pure Rust, +546 stars today), both by storytold, launched within the last ~11 days and picking up fast momentum. gitdiscover.org

4. Cybersecurity

  • ConnectWise suspends Cloud Backup and takes SaaS Security/Cloud Management offline (developing — escalation of the SkyKick story) — after attackers compromised M365 customer tenants via ConnectWise app identities (discovered Oct 14; Huntress confirmed 173 affected tenants), ConnectWise disabled 7 application identities at 07:00 UTC Oct 17, revoked certificates, and suspended scheduled backups and restore access; archives said encrypted and untouched. Credential-exposure mechanism still under investigation. The Hacker News
  • Cysic halted its crypto bridge after an unauthorized 10M CYS token mint on BNB Smart Chain (Oct 18, developing) — incident scoped to bridge message processing/minting; no theft of existing CYS or wallet assets claimed; final dollar loss under assessment; security firm hired, follow-up report promised. Traders Union
  • Overnight ransomware CLAIMS (all unverified, tracker reports) — Settra published 51GB from Acclaim Companies (US professional services; leak includes executive SSNs via IRS Form SS-4); N0n listed SQA Services Inc (software QA test lab for Wolters Kluwer/CCH Axcess; VPN creds for 64 employee accounts exposed); Settra claims 950GB exfiltrated from exo.inc including patient data (attack dated Sept 30). QPulse · HookPhish · QPulse
  • New practical write-up on AhsayCBS exploitation (Oct 18) — step-by-step exposure check for CVE-2026-105134/CVE-2026-105133 (auth bypass + command injection in backup console, already in CISA KEV): inventory internet-facing consoles below v10.3.4, rotate repository credentials after patching, verify the restore path. Not a new vuln — a new response resource. dev.to

Exploitation ongoing, not yet in CISA KEV. Tech-Insider
