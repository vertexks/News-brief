1. World escalation

Energy ceasefire already broken, per Kyiv — Kharkiv CHP-5 and a Kyiv plant hit after Trump's post (developing)

Zelensky's evening address said Russian drones/glides kept coming all day Sunday: Kharkiv's CHP-5 was hit at least twice and caught fire, and a Shahed hit a Kyiv power plant causing outages in many districts. Kyiv says Russia made 200+ strikes on power generation/transmission in the past week alone. Moscow still hasn't commented on the ceasefire terms.

Source: https://www.wvlt.tv/2026/10/11/trump-says-russia-ukraine-agree-an-energy-ceasefire-kyiv-says-attacks-continue/ and https://investinglive.com/commodities/gold-edges-up-in-asia-on-the-energy-truce-narrative-that-looks-like-a-very-shaky-support/

Sunday Riyadh airport attack toll: 1 killed, 11 injured — terminals 3 and 4 hit

The Times puts a toll on Sunday's second-day strike, which the 5 PM brief only had as fire/injuries at Terminal 4. That's the 4th Houthi targeting of the airport in a week. King Fahd Airport in Dammam was also targeted Sunday evening, with damage assessments under way, and the UK Foreign Office now advises avoiding all Saudi airports except Medina, plus Jeddah and Dammam.

Source: https://www.thetimes.com/world/middle-east/article/attack-saudi-arabia-airport-houthi-lx2q8kvmn

New late-Sunday Hormuz incident: drone hits tanker near Sohar, Oman — crew evacuating, 2 reported missing (developing)

UKMTO's 21:02 UTC update confirmed a tanker near the Sohar anchorage was hit by a UAV, local authorities were helping evacuate the crew, and damage/environmental impact were unknown. TradeWinds identifies it as the products tanker Petro Dolphin and says 2 of its 11 Indian seafarers are reported missing. This is separate from the earlier Sunday outbound-tanker projectile strike where the crew was safe. TradeWinds counts 20 vessels struck since Sep 29.

Source: https://www.tradewindsnews.com/casualties/ukmto-confirms-drone-attack-off-oman-as-hormuz-crisis-deepens/2-1-2056917 and https://www.democrata.es/en/international/a-drone-crashes-into-an-oil-tanker-off-the-coast-of-oman-in-the-strait-of-hormuz/

Oil up on the weekend's attacks; Goldman raised its December Brent forecast to $90

Reuters' Monday-market wrap ties the move to the Houthi escalation plus the two weekend tanker attacks in/near Hormuz.

Source: https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/oil-climbs-mideast-conflict-tanker-attacks-persist-2026-10-11/

Saudi Arabia lines up partners; US still holding back from joining strikes

Saudi Arabia, Egypt, Pakistan and Turkey issued a joint Sunday condemnation of the Houthi airport attacks. CNN's Sunday analysis adds France, Britain and Pakistan have committed support and Turkey is considering help, while the US has so far sent 100+ advisers for intelligence under a new joint command rather than joining airstrikes — despite MBS asking Trump twice last month.

Source: https://newsbreaktv.com/turkiye-egypt-pakistan-saudi-arabia/ and https://www.cnn.com/2026/10/11/world/saudi-arabia-airport-attack-analysis-intl?cid=external-feeds_iluminar_meta

A Sunday news roundup reports police detained a man in his 60s from Lower Silesia suspected of preparing the threatened attack on Tusk and Kosiniak-Kamysz, identified through online content. The public tool flags SynthID watermarks in AI media, including output from partners like OpenAI and Nvidia. Coverage on Oct 11 notes the key limit plainly: it detects watermarks, not AI in general, and accuracy drops on heavily processed/compressed files.

FT reporting from Oct 10, carried Oct 11: Nvidia is discussing a full acquisition, a deeper investment, or an acqui-hire/licensing arrangement for open-weight model maker Reflection AI, in which it has already invested $800M. Talks are early and could collapse — not a confirmed deal or product.

Source: https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/nvidia-looks-to-purchase-open-weight-ai-firm-reflection/ and https://tech-insider.org/nvidia-reflection-ai-acquihire-800-million-2026/

The MIT-licensed reverse-engineering agent (one MCP server in front of Ghidra/Hopper/IDA and other tools; npx rea-agents setup) was in this morning's brief as a trending project — the new part is today's 6.4.0 release and the star count, plus pushback from working reverse engineers asking what's genuinely new beyond the wrapper.

Source: https://undercodenews.com/a11ign-just-updated-versions-090-and-0101-strengthen-authenticated-security-scanning-video/

4. Cybersecurity

Two high-severity auth-bypass CVEs disclosed Oct 11

  • CVE-2026-108718 — Rill 0.77.0–0.90.5: its admin OAuth server issues authorization codes to dynamically registered clients without user consent. CVSS 8.1.
  • CVE-2026-108746 — Vearch 3.5.2–3.5.9: Role.HasPermissionForResources ignores stored ReadOnly/None privilege levels, an authorization bypass. CVSS 8.8.

Neither has confirmed public exploit code or a CISA KEV listing yet — patch proactively if you run either.

Source: https://www.thehackerwire.com/vulnerability/CVE-2026-108718/ and https://www.thehackerwire.com/vulnerability/CVE-2026-108746/
