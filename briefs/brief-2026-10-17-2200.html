4. Cybersecurity

Poisoned AOMEI updates: attackers hijacked 5–10-minute update windows to push backdoors (UNC7017 reporting)

Attackers tampered with AOMEI's update mechanism in brief windows, delivering backdoors — meaning update infrastructure that looks clean afterward doesn't rule out endpoint compromise. Single-source so far, moderate confidence.

https://churchofmalware.org/news/2026-10-17-connectwise-breaches-and-poisoned-aomei-updates/

Security firm Calif published full root privesc PoC for OpenBSD 7.9

Chains an integer overflow in shmat(4)'s 16-bit shared-memory attach counter (inherited from 1988 BSD) with a kernel refcount use-after-free; bypasses KARL, SMAP, SMEP. Fixed by errata 018 (Sept 14) and 026 (Sept 30) via syspatch — patch status is what matters. No CVE assigned, no in-the-wild exploitation reported. Local-only attack.

https://cyberrecaps.com/news/cybersecurity-news-october-17-2026

Japan's breach wave keeps growing — week-in-review tally

ShopServe (23.36M people), SourceNext (22.52M), Burger King Japan app (11.18M) confirmed taken; disaster-alert service Arcadia (3.14M emails) breached — a service sending emergency alerts for hundreds of municipalities. Also: ransomware group claimed a major Tokyo railway group; JPCERT confirmed attackers' use of AI.
