Night brief — Tuesday Oct 13, 2026 (~10 PM CT)

Sweep window: last ~5 hours (since the 5 PM CT brief). Big night: the energy "ceasefire" is effectively dead on both sides, and the Houthis hit Aramco's headquarters in Dhahran.

1. World escalation

  • Russia hit Kyiv with ballistic missiles Tuesday evening, struck thermal power plant No. 4. Zelensky says Ukraine got no advance warning from Western partners — second night in a row — and that "the facts show that the Russians do not consider themselves bound to abide by what the president of the United States said." One person confirmed killed in a burning apartment building; Trump denied intelligence sharing had stopped. (Reuters, Guardian war briefing via Europe Says)
  • Single-source, unverified: Ukraine reportedly struck a Russian oil depot in Valuyki (Belgorod Oblast) overnight with 500+ drones — would be the first Ukrainian energy strike since Trump's ceasefire announcement; Kyiv reportedly answered "we will burn Russian refineries." US had warned it could cut intel sharing and freeze PURL if Kyiv resumed energy strikes. Developing, treat with caution. (BritBrief, BritBrief follow-up)
  • Houthi ballistic missile hit Saudi Aramco's headquarters complex in Dhahran on Tuesday night — an office tower, top three floors damaged, building set on fire (later put out). Saudi Energy Ministry confirmed the hit; no injuries. This is the first strike to reach Aramco's HQ campus — the administrative heart of the Saudi oil industry. (Reuters via Philippine Daily Post, Bloomberg via Mint)
  • Coalition said it intercepted three Houthi cruise missiles and two drones Tuesday over Riyadh and the south; four people injured in Riyadh by falling debris. (JFeed)
  • Trump on Truth Social: Houthis "behaving very badly," probably at Iran's direction to push oil prices before the Nov 3 midterms — said he thought they'd learned their lesson from last year's US bombing and warned "I will never forget." (Seoul Economic Daily, NY Post)
  • Houthis say Saudi-led coalition hit them in Yemen on Tuesday — airstrikes on Kamaran island (Red Sea), Qanawis district near Hodeida, and Jawf province; rebels also claim they shot down a Saudi drone over Saada. No Saudi comment yet. (AP via Reflector)
  • Poland: no fresh developments tonight; Krzysztof N. custody ruling is Wednesday.

3. Open source

  • Ruflo (Cognitum One) shipped a new release today — open-source AI agent meta-harness / multi-agent orchestration for Claude Code and Codex via MCP: "Ruflo Mods" turn one mission into an end-to-end research→build→test→secure workflow, plus a single control plane for agent swarms. 74k GitHub stars, 12.5M npm downloads in the last year. Worth a look if you're playing with Claude Code. (Morningstar/AccessWire)
  • Janas — open-source project that streams LLM model experts from SSD, so models bigger than your RAM can still run locally. Genuinely useful concept for local AI. (dev.to Dev News Digest)
