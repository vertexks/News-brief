1. Ukraine/Russia + Poland

Putin agrees "in principle" to trilateral talks after Nov. 10 — but not at the leaders' table

The readout from the Witkoff–Putin meeting finally landed this afternoon: Kremlin foreign-policy adviser Yuri Ushakov said Putin agreed "in principle" that trilateral US–Russia–Ukraine talks will continue after Nov. 10, focused on "building a security system for Russia and Ukraine after the conflict ends." A US official added the talks would happen "at the technical level" — meaning Putin himself won't sit down with Trump or Zelensky. Both sides accepted the trilateral format (Ukraine in Oslo Tuesday, Russia in St. Petersburg today), and the US readout cited "progress" on a post-war economic plan and European security architecture. The catch, per CNN: no indication Russia has budged on its maximalist positions — full Donbas, a shrunken Ukrainian military, no NATO. Russian envoy Kirill Dmitriev separately said the US–Russia diesel deal goes ahead despite European criticism. CNN · NY Post

Zelensky: "freeze and stop" — he'd accept a deal that freezes the current front lines

In a Fox & Friends interview Wednesday morning, Zelensky said yes to a peace deal that freezes the fighting where it stands, with Ukraine keeping "75% or more" of its territory: "Right now we have to freeze, and I think this is (the) real way to end this war. We call it a ceasefire… freeze and stop." He called that the minimum needed from Russia, followed by US and European security guarantees so "the occupation will not return." He drew a line at giving up the Donbas — "not fair" — and at any deal negotiated without Ukraine ("don't speak about us without us"). It's a notable shift from his long-held rejection of frozen conflicts, and it landed hours before Putin's own "in principle" answer. NY Post · CNN

Hubbard freed and heading to a Texas military hospital

The one concrete outcome of the St. Petersburg meeting: 74-year-old retired teacher Stephen Hubbard, detained since April 2022 and sentenced as an alleged mercenary, was released and photographed with Witkoff at the airport. CBS reported he will fly to Texas for medical treatment at a military hospital; he is in poor health after more than four years in Russian custody. NY Post

Poland: quiet — no fresh developments; Krzysztof N. remains in 2-month pretrial detention.

2. Iran/US + Yemen/Saudi + Hormuz

Iran–US deadlock, stated plainly by both sides: no talks under blockade, no relief while ships are attacked

Iranian President Pezeshkian said Wednesday the US "cannot expect dialogue" while imposing a blockade and issuing threats. Foreign Minister Araghchi, in a Financial Times op-ed, said Iran would give diplomacy "another chance" if it sees "strong evidence of a sincere desire" for a just solution — and urged a return to the June Islamabad memorandum, under which Iran would reopen the strait and the US would lift the blockade, release frozen oil funds and remove sanctions. Washington isn't biting: Secretary of State Rubio told US media a deal would be "extremely difficult" and economic pressure continues until Iran stops targeting commercial shipping. Indirect backchannels via Pakistan and Qatar are reportedly still active, but nothing is confirmed. Bloomberg via Military.com · AnewZ

Shaybah oilfield: Saudi defences intercepted a drone — and one outlet reports two killed

Saudi Arabia's Energy Ministry said air defences intercepted a drone attempting to hit a facility at Aramco's Shaybah oilfield in the Eastern Region. A breaking-news outlet separately reported two people killed in a drone attack on Shaybah with no operational impact — that death toll is single-source and unconfirmed, so treat it as developing. Shaybah is one of the sites the Houthis claimed to have hit Monday; Saudi sources denied those claims, saying the facilities are intact. for-ex.news (intercept + reported deaths)

Oil: Brent holding near $110 as attacks spread beyond the strait

Brent rose to $109.64 early Wednesday (WTI $98.58) after settling Tuesday at $108.79, off a five-month high of $111.99. LSEG analysts note attacks have expanded beyond Hormuz into the Gulf of Oman, threatening loadings outside the Gulf and the ship-to-ship transfers that have kept some exports moving. One single-source report says the US authorized offloading cargo from some blocked vessels, which eased prices late in the day — unconfirmed, developing. Reuters

3. Tech: Apple, Nvidia, Google

Google Cloud's Vera Rubin A5X instances go live — general availability in November, up to 960,000 Rubin GPUs planned

Announced at GTC Berlin on Wednesday: the first live A5X instances, built on Nvidia Vera Rubin NVL72 racks with ConnectX-9 SuperNICs, Google's Virgo Network and fourth-generation liquid cooling, are running with an initial customer — Ineffable Intelligence, the London lab founded by AlphaGo creator David Silver, which is using them for reinforcement-learning systems that learn from experience. General availability comes next month, with expansion to Europe planned, and Google Cloud says it plans to scale Vera Rubin infrastructure to as many as 960,000 GPUs across multiple sites (a stated plan, not installed capacity). WPP also demoed a creative-agent reference architecture on A5X with Nvidia Nemotron models and OpenShell, with multi-node workloads running on a single instance in initial tests. Unite.AI · Tesla News Wire

Apple confirms "limited" iPhone Duo walk-in stock for Friday — and Gurman calls the preorder timing a "major mistake"

Apple told MacRumors and 9to5Mac that stores will have limited Duo units for walk-in customers on Friday's Oct. 23 launch in the US, Australia, Austria, Belgium, Canada, Mexico, Germany, the Netherlands, Singapore, Spain, Sweden, Switzerland, Türkiye, the UK and most stores in Italy (Macao: launch-day walk-ins only, limited). No walk-in sales in France, China, Hong Kong, India, Japan, Malaysia, South Korea, Taiwan, Thailand or the UAE — reservation-only pickup there. Apple expects the limited stock to sell out quickly; AppleInsider reports new pre-orders are now showing January 25, 2027 delivery. Bloomberg's Mark Gurman, in his Power On newsletter, called launching pre-orders Oct. 16 despite known "severe supply constraints" a "major mistake."

Follow-up: DeepInfer keeps climbing — ~17,500 stars by Wednesday evening Berlin time

DeepSeek's open-sourced production inference engine, covered in the morning brief at 14,601 stars, was at roughly 17,500 stars and ~1,800 forks by Wednesday evening, per a GitHub API check cited in an independent write-up. The caveats from the morning brief still stand: no independent benchmarks yet, and the release omits a KV-cache system, prefill/decode disaggregation and replica load balancing. djcroman

Railtracks Swift ADK goes open source under MIT

Railtown AI open-sourced its Railtracks Swift agent-development kit on GitHub under the MIT licence, extending its existing Python framework into Apple's native environment. It supports agent reasoning, tool invocation, structured outputs, multi-agent orchestration, execution tracing and guardrails, with OpenAI, Anthropic and Google Gemini providers plus on-device Apple Intelligence. Company announcement — adoption is the thing to watch, not the press release. Kalkine

5. Cybersecurity

PHP2Shell: emergency patches out today for two critical PHP flaws — NSA, FBI and CISA say patch now or take systems offline

PHP released fixes Wednesday (8.5.12, 8.4.27, 8.3.36, 8.2.35) for CVE-2026-109618 (RCE via out-of-memory corruption) and CVE-2026-109619 (RCE via stale global state), both CVSS 9.2, both allowing unauthenticated remote code execution. The release was pulled forward a day after details leaked on Hacker News ahead of the planned Oct. 22 disclosure. Apache mod_php is at highest risk — PHP says a demonstrated exploit triggers before application code runs — but FPM, CGI, FrankenPHP and LiteSpeed are also at risk. NSA, FBI and CISA urged immediate patching or taking unpatched systems offline; Unit 42 counts 22.6M+ internet-exposed instances potentially vulnerable. Cloudflare says it is already seeing early attack attempts and has deployed WAF rules; watchTowr honeypots logged exploitation attempts; several European government sites went offline to patch. CISA had not observed exploitation as of publication, and neither CVE is on KEV yet — but exploit code is circulating publicly, so this is a patch-now, not patch-soon, item. Defense One · SC Media · Tenable FAQ

LiteLLM 1.107.0 was hijacked on PyPI for ~1¾ hours — a credential stealer in one of AI's most-used plumbing packages

BerriAI confirmed its LiteLLM 1.107.0 release, published to PyPI and Docker on Wednesday, "contained compiled malware which steals credentials and loads remote-code execution tools." Attackers used three engineers' GitHub accounts via apparently stolen GitHub CLI tokens, overrode branch protection, and built the release from a lookalike "Berri-AI" copy of the repo. Seven malicious wheels were live on PyPI 11:39–13:23 UTC; tainted Docker images (including latest) stayed up until 14:20 UTC. The payload steals SSH keys, cloud credentials, CI/CD secrets, browser passwords and LLM provider keys, and runs on any Python startup — even without importing litellm. Wiz already observed stolen AWS credentials being validated from 16:20 UTC, consistent with LLMjacking. If you installed or ran 1.107.0, rotate everything it could have touched; 1.106.2 and the release candidates are clean. This is LiteLLM's second hijack this year. BerriAI incident report · Wiz · Socket

6. Major court cases

Russell Brand trial: final complainant's recorded interview played — "you treated me like an animal"

At Southwark Crown Court on Wednesday, jurors heard the recorded police interview of the final complainant, who alleges Brand anally raped her at his home in 2009 after consensual sex began. She described a later text exchange, about six months after the alleged incident, in which she told him he had "treated her like an animal" and he replied "I'm sorry" — and said she was left feeling "really degraded" and "like a piece of meat." Brand's lawyer put it to her that she had agreed to the act, suggesting she was "young, impressionable and obsessed" with him. Brand denies all seven charges (3 rapes, 3 sexual assaults, 1 indecent assault, 1999–2009); the ~6-week trial continues. Reuters via Devdiscourse · The Times live blog

Netanyahu trial: Mandelblit testimony continued into day 4, no major new English-language developments

A Hebrew-language summary indicates the fourth day of former AG Mandelblit's defense testimony proceeded Wednesday, covering investigator conduct and authorization questions; no major new development surfaced in English-language reporting by brief time. Testimony is scheduled through Thursday.
